# Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Model Architecture Specification

This document details the architectural parameters, dimension shapes, and operation-level
specifications for **`Qwen/Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-FP8`** and **`Qwen/Qwen3.5-35B-A3B`** (BF16). All
symbols follow [NOTATION.md](../../../NOTATION.md) for use in roofline performance estimation.

**Sources.** Every hyperparameter below is read from the official checkpoint's `config.json`,
fetched from `https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-FP8/raw/main/config.json`.
Layer structure and fusion layout follow the vLLM implementation:

*   `vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_5.py` — `Qwen3_5DecoderLayer`, `Qwen3_5Model`
*   `vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_next.py` — `Qwen3NextAttention`, `Qwen3NextSparseMoeBlock`
*   `vllm/model_executor/layers/mamba/gdn/qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py` — `QwenGatedDeltaNetAttention`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scope: the text decoder.** Like `Qwen3.5-397B-A17B`, the checkpoint is multimodal (`qwen3_5_moe`)
> and ships a Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) head (`mtp_num_hidden_layers: 1`); neither is on the
> text-only serving path unless explicitly enabled. See [§6](#6-out-of-scope-modules-vision-tower--mtp-head).

## 2. Layer Structure & Tensor Flow

Qwen3.5-35B-A3B is a **hybrid linear/softmax attention** model in which *every* layer is MoE.

*   **Attention sub-layers (3:1 interleaving, `full_attention_interval = 4`):**
    *   3 GDN linear-attention layers followed by 1 gated GQA layer, repeated 10 times across 40 layers.
    *   **GQA layers (0-indexed, 10 total):** $\{3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39\}$, i.e. every layer with $\ell \bmod 4 = 3$.
    *   **GDN layers (0-indexed, 30 total):** all remaining layers.
    *   *Verified directly against the explicit 40-entry `layer_types` list in `config.json`.*
*   **FFN sub-layers:** all 40 layers are `Qwen3NextSparseMoeBlock` — Top-8 of 256 routed experts
    ($F_{\text{expert}} = 512$) **plus** 1 always-on shared expert
    ($F_{\text{shared}} = 512$) gated by a scalar sigmoid (`shared_expert_gate`).
*   **Residual/norm:** standard pre-norm. `input_layernorm` (RMSNorm) before attention,
    `post_attention_layernorm` (RMSNorm) before the MoE block, plain additive residuals.

## 3. Sub-Component Operations & Tensor Shapes

### 3.1. Gated Delta Network (GDN) Sub-Layer — 30 Layers

Refer to the [GDN Roofline Skill](../../common/attention/gdn/SKILL.md) for the operational
formulas. Note that $D_k = D_v = 128$, $H_{qk} = 16$, and $H_v = 32$ ($H_v / H_{qk} = 2$).

#### 1. QKV Projection (`in_proj_qkv`)

*   Weight: $[D,\ 2 H_{qk} D_k + H_v D_v] = [2048,\ 8192]$. Column-parallel.
*   Splits as `q` (2,048) | `k` (2,048) | `v` (4,096) — exactly $D_{\text{conv}} = 8{,}192$.
*   **FLOPs:** $2 B_{\text{local}} S \cdot D \cdot (2 H_{qk} D_k + H_v D_v)$.

#### 2. Z / B / A Gate Projections (`in_proj_z`, `in_proj_b`, `in_proj_a`)

Combined column-parallel width $H_v D_v + 2 H_v = 4{,}096 + 64 = 4{,}160$:

*   **`in_proj_z`** (RMSNorm gate): $[D,\ H_v D_v] = [2048,\ 4096]$.
*   **`in_proj_b`** (write rate $\beta$): $[D,\ H_v] = [2048,\ 32]$ (`bf16`).
*   **`in_proj_a`** (decay $\alpha$): $[D,\ H_v] = [2048,\ 32]$ (`bf16`).
*   **FLOPs:** $2 B_{\text{local}} S \cdot D \cdot (H_v D_v + 2 H_v)$.

#### 3. Causal Depthwise 1D Convolution + SiLU

Refer to the [Conv1D Roofline Skill](../../common/conv1d/SKILL.md).

*   $D_{\text{conv}} = 8{,}192$, $K_{\text{conv}} = 4$, SiLU activation, depthwise
    (`groups = D_conv`). Weight $[D_{\text{conv}}, 1, K_{\text{conv}}] = [8192, 1, 4]$ in `bf16`.
*   **Prefill FLOPs:** $2 B_{\text{local}} S \cdot D_{\text{conv}} \cdot K_{\text{conv}}$.
*   **Decode conv state:** $[B_{\text{local}},\ K_{\text{conv}} - 1,\ D_{\text{conv}}] = [B_{\text{local}}, 3, 8192]$.

#### 4. Gated Delta Rule Kernel

Applied after **L2 normalization of $q$ and $k$** (`l2norm_fwd`). See the GDN skill
[§4](../../common/attention/gdn/SKILL.md) for prefill (chunk-wise) and decode (recurrent) formulas.

*   **Recurrent state:** $[B_{\text{local}},\ H_v,\ D_k,\ D_v] = [B_{\text{local}}, 32, 128, 128]$,
    held in **`float32`** (`mamba_ssm_dtype: "float32"`) — $2.0\text{ MiB}$ ($2.097\text{ MB}$) per sequence per layer.
*   **Decode FLOPs:** $7 B_{\text{local}} H_v D_k D_v \approx 3.67\ B_{\text{local}}\text{ MFLOPs}$ per layer per step.

#### 5. Gated RMSNorm

*   `RMSNormGated(head_v_dim=128)` with **SiLU** activation over $[B_{\text{local}}, S, 4096]$.

#### 6. Output Projection (`out_proj`)

Refer to the [Output Projection Skill](../../common/attention/out_proj/SKILL.md).

*   Weight: $[H_v D_v,\ D] = [4096,\ 2048]$. Row-parallel → **TP All-Reduce**.
*   **FLOPs:** $2 B_{\text{local}} S \cdot H_v D_v \cdot D$.

### 3.2. Gated Full Attention (GQA) Sub-Layer — 10 Layers

Refer to the [QKV Projection](../../common/attention/qkv_proj/SKILL.md),
[Global Attention](../../common/attention/global_attention/SKILL.md) and
[Output Projection](../../common/attention/out_proj/SKILL.md) skills.

*   **Fused QKV + Gate Projection (`qkv_proj`):** $[2048, 9216]$ (`q` 4096 | `gate` 4096 | `k` 512 | `v` 512).
*   **Causal Attention:** $H_q = 16, H_{kv} = 2, D_h = 256, D_{\text{rope}} = 64$.
*   **Output Projection (`o_proj`):** $[4096, 2048]$.

### 3.3. Sparse MoE Sub-Layer — All 40 Layers

Refer to the [MoE Roofline Skill](../../common/moe/SKILL.md).

*   **Router (`mlp.gate`):** $[2048, 256]$ (`bf16`), Top-$K = 8$.
*   **Routed Experts (256 experts, Top-8, SwiGLU):** $F_{\text{expert}} = 512$, `W1` $[2048, 1024]$, `W2` $[512, 2048]$.
*   **Shared Expert (1, always active):** $F_{\text{shared}} = 512$, gated by `shared_expert_gate` $[2048, 1]$ (`bf16`).

## 4. Precision, Quantization & Memory Footprint

1.  **GQA KV cache (10 layers):** $10{,}240 \cdot b_{\text{kv}}\text{ B/token}$ ($10.0\text{ KiB/tok}$ in fp8, $20.0\text{ KiB/tok}$ in bf16).
2.  **GDN recurrent state (30 layers):** $30 \cdot 32 \cdot 128 \cdot 128 \cdot 4\text{ B} = 62.91\text{ MB}$ ($60.0\text{ MiB}$) per sequence.
3.  **GDN conv state (30 layers):** $30 \cdot 3 \cdot 8192 \cdot 2\text{ B} = 1.47\text{ MB}$ ($1.41\text{ MiB}$) per sequence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Parameter Count Derivation

*   30 GDN layers: $1.012\text{ B}$
*   10 GQA layers: $0.273\text{ B}$
*   40 MoE routed blocks: $32.212\text{ B}$
*   40 MoE shared + router + gate: $0.147\text{ B}$
*   `embed_tokens` + `lm_head`: $1.017\text{ B}$
*   **Text decoder total:** $\mathbf{34.66\text{ B}}$ (+ $0.44\text{ B}$ vision tower $= \mathbf{35.10\text{ B}}$).
*   **Active parameters per token:** $\mathbf{2.95\text{ B}}$ GEMM ($3.46\text{ B}$ with `embed_tokens`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Out-of-Scope Modules: Vision Tower & MTP Head

Vision tower ($\approx 0.44\text{ B}$) and 1-layer MTP head ($\approx 0.84\text{ B}$) are omitted on standard text-only serving paths.
